YOUR QUANTUM STARTER KIT

Big ideas, made simple.

Start with the essentials. Each concept includes a clear explanation and a practical way to think about it.

|0⟩
CONCEPT 01

Qubits

A qubit is a unit of quantum information. Its state can combine the basis states |0⟩ and |1⟩.

✳
CONCEPT 02

Superposition

A quantum state can be a combination of possibilities; measurement outcomes follow probabilities.

H
CONCEPT 03

Quantum gates

Gates transform quantum states. They are the building blocks used to construct quantum circuits.

⤳
CONCEPT 04

Measurement

Measurement returns a classical result from a quantum system, with probabilities determined by its state.

∞
CONCEPT 05

Entanglement

Entangled systems can share correlations that cannot be described as independent states of each part.

⌘
CONCEPT 06

Quantum circuits

A circuit arranges gates and measurements into a sequence that can be simulated or run on hardware.
